Enter the amount per pay, not annual salary. For $60,000/year paid monthly, enter $5,000.

Year-to-date amounts and tax settings

Enter amounts BEFORE this pay, from this employer only. Leave zero for the first pay of the year or a new employer. Do not include another employer's deductions.

Enter 0 in both TD1 claim fields when no personal amounts can be claimed. A blank federal claim uses the income-adjusted basic amount. No additional Ontario dependant reduction is included.

Includes employee deductions plus employer contributions. Take-home pay + CRA remittance = salary + employer CPP/EI. This is an amount calculation, not a remittance due-date calculation.

Scope and assumptions

2026 regular salary in Ontario, all salary pensionable and insurable, employee age 18–64 throughout the year and CPP-eligible for all 12 months. Standard employer EI multiplier of 1.4. Other ages, CPP elections, exemptions, bonuses, taxable benefits, pensions, RRSP/union deductions and province transfers require a different calculation.

Employer cost shown covers salary and employer CPP/EI only; it excludes WSIB, employer health tax, vacation pay added separately, benefits and other employment costs. Income tax is estimated withholding, not the employee's final annual tax bill.

Uses CRA T4127 Option 1, including Ontario surtax and health premium. Verify against CRA's payroll calculator before processing actual payroll. This tool has not been independently certified or checked against PDOC.
